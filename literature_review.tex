\chapter{Literature Review}
\label{ch:litreview}

\section{Small modular PWRs and naval-heritage cores}
\label{sec:lit-smr}

The pressurised-water SMR family provides the reference frame for a
LABGENE-class core.

The NuScale design is the most completely documented. Its NuFuel-HTP2
assembly is a reduced-height $17\times17$ lattice with UO$_2$ enriched
up to 4.95\,wt\% and gadolinia homogeneously mixed into selected
pellets, analysed in the licensing basis up to 8\,wt\%
Gd$_2$O$_3$~\cite{nuscale_htp2_2017}. Open academic reconstructions of
a NuScale-like core, with 264 fuel rods, 24 guide tubes and one
instrumentation tube at 1.26\,cm pitch, provide a benchmark geometry
directly comparable to the present model~\cite{nuscale_like_core_2024,
ez_aldeen_2025}.

Boron-free operation, the natural regime for a compact naval plant,
sharpens the burnable-absorber problem. The Korean innovative-SMR
programme designs soluble-boron-free assemblies with twenty gadolinia
rods, exploiting isotopically enriched gadolinium to extend suppression
across long cycles~\cite{ismr_sbf_2024,higa_2024}. The Chinese ACP100
documents a boron-free variant in which control rods and burnable
poison provide the whole of reactivity
compensation~\cite{acp100_physor2020}.

A Rolls-Royce-like SMR study of advanced-technology fuels is of
particular methodological relevance. It fixes the gadolinia
concentration at 8.0\,wt\% and treats the \emph{number} of poisoned
rods per assembly, from 20 to 40, as the design
variable~\cite{rr_smr_atf_2025}. High-burnup PWR core studies extend
the same fuel platform to a 75\,MWd/kgU peak-pin target with up to
9\,wt\% gadolinia~\cite{highburnup_core_2024}, the discharge limit
adopted in this thesis.

\section{Gadolinia burnable absorbers}
\label{sec:lit-gd}

Gadolinia owes its role to the extreme thermal absorption of $^{155}$Gd
and $^{157}$Gd, which renders poisoned pellets optically black and
makes the absorber strongly self-shielding. Suppression duration is
therefore governed by surface burn-off rather than bulk content, and
industrial practice concentrates the poison in a limited number of rods
precisely to exploit this effect~\cite{gd_enriched_2021}.

Operating experience is extensive, of the order of 45\,000 gadolinia
rods across 29 light-water reactors by the late
1980s~\cite{anf_gd_1987}, and includes concentrations up to 10\,wt\% in
commercial reloads with reduced uranium enrichment in the poisoned
rods~\cite{robinson_gd10}.

Two systematic effects recur across the literature and matter to the
present work.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Gadolinia rods tend to \emph{raise} the local peaking factor by
        depressing their own power and loading their
        neighbours~\cite{ap1000_gd_2022}.

  \item The end-of-life residual penalty of natural gadolinia is small,
        below $0.2\%\,\Delta k$ near 45\,GWd/MTU~\cite{ba_snf_2001}.
\end{enumerate}

Parametric studies quantify the count--concentration equivalence. In an
APR1400-type lattice, four rods at $\sim$3.3\,wt\% match eight rods at
$\sim$7.2\,wt\% for the same BOL
reactivity~\cite{apr1400_gd_2020}. Enriched-gadolinium variants extend
the design space further~\cite{gd_enriched_2021,ap1000_gd_2022}. A
parametric study of enriched gadolinium exists specifically for the
Angra-2 assembly~\cite{angra2_gd_2018}, the domestic heritage plant of
the present design basis.

\section{Surrogate-assisted multi-objective core optimisation}
\label{sec:lit-moo}

Direct coupling of a depletion solver to a population-based optimiser
is computationally prohibitive, which motivates the surrogate-assisted
pattern adopted here: a cheap statistical model is trained on a design
of experiments, an evolutionary algorithm searches the surrogate, and a
small number of proposed designs are evaluated with the expensive code
in an active-learning loop.

The components of that pattern are standard.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item NSGA-II~\cite{deb_nsga2_2002} remains the standard
        multi-objective engine.

  \item Gaussian-process regression provides the surrogate together
        with a principled predictive
        uncertainty~\cite{rasmussen_gp_2006}.

  \item Latin hypercube sampling supplies the space-filling initial
        design~\cite{mckay_lhs_1979}.
\end{enumerate}

In reactor applications, Whyte and Parks demonstrated NSGA-II with
neural-network surrogates for PWR loading
design~\cite{whyte_parks_2021}, and the dual-stage active-learning
architecture that seeded the present pipeline was formulated in a
Karlsruhe Institute of Technology work
proposal~\cite{kit_inr_proposal}. OpenMC supplies the transport and
depletion engine as a fully open-source Monte Carlo
code~\cite{romano_openmc_2015}.

\section{Noisy objectives and selection under uncertainty}
\label{sec:lit-noise}

Monte Carlo objectives place the optimisation in the noisy-evaluation
regime, for which two bodies of theory are relevant and, in the
reactor-design literature, rarely applied together.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item An objective defined as the \emph{maximum} of many noisy
        estimates is an order statistic and inherits the well-known
        upward bias of extreme values. The winning cell is
        preferentially one that fluctuated high, the bias grows as the
        underlying field flattens, and it shrinks only with the
        per-cell variance.

  \item Identifying the best of several noisy alternatives is the
        classical ranking-and-selection problem. Gupta-style subset
        selection retains every candidate whose confidence interval
        overlaps the incumbent's and discards the rest with controlled
        error~\cite{gupta_1965}. Common random numbers are the standard
        variance-reduction device for paired comparison, though their
        benefit vanishes when the compared systems decorrelate, as the
        present work finds for designs of different lattice pitch.
\end{enumerate}

The gap this thesis addresses sits at the intersection.
Surrogate-assisted MOO studies of reactor cores generally treat the
Monte Carlo objective as deterministic at fixed seed, leaving both the
estimator bias and the ranking reliability of the resulting Pareto
front unexamined.

\section{Multi-fidelity optimisation}
\label{sec:lit-mf}

When the objective is evaluated by models of different fidelity, the
canonical statistical fusion is the Kennedy--O'Hagan autoregressive
model~\cite{kennedy_ohagan_2000}.

\begin{equation}
Z_\mathrm{hi}(x) = \rho\, Z_\mathrm{lo}(x) + \delta(x)
\label{eq:kennedy_ohagan}
\end{equation}

\noindent where the symbols have the following meaning.

\begin{itemize}
  \item $Z_\mathrm{hi}(x)$ is the high-fidelity response at design $x$,
        in the units of the modelled quantity.

  \item $Z_\mathrm{lo}(x)$ is the low-fidelity response at the same
        design, in the same units.

  \item $\rho$ is the autoregressive scale factor of the model,
        dimensionless. It is not the reactivity of
        Equation~\eqref{eq:reactivity}, with which it shares the
        symbol in the source literature.

  \item $\delta(x)$ is the discrepancy term, modelled as a Gaussian
        process, in the same units as the responses.

  \item $x$ is the design vector.
\end{itemize}

Forrester et al.\ give the engineering recipe and demonstrate
optimisation on few high-fidelity samples~\cite{forrester_2007}.
Trust-region model management optimises a corrected low-fidelity model
with provable convergence to the high-fidelity
optimum~\cite{alexandrov_2001}. Multi-fidelity Bayesian optimisation
selects design \emph{and} fidelity jointly per unit
cost~\cite{poloczek_miso_2017,kandasamy_boca_2017}. Nuclear
applications include machine-learning correction of low-fidelity nodal
solutions against Monte Carlo benchmarks~\cite{vver_ml_2026}.

A recurring caution motivates the present work's route. Low-fidelity to
high-fidelity correlation weakens near the optimum, exactly where an
optimiser converges. This thesis therefore measures the high-fidelity
quantity in-loop where its cost permits, rather than correcting the
proxy.

\section{Loading patterns and power peaking}
\label{sec:lit-lp}

Commercial cores reach their $F_{\Delta H}$ targets largely through
reload design. Simulated-annealing loading-pattern optimisation with
peaking as an explicit objective reduced maximum radial peaking by
6.1\% at fixed fuel inventory~\cite{kropaczek_turinsky_1991}. Even
first cores obtain flatness from heterogeneous batch placement,
optimised to minimise radial peaking under cycle-length
constraints~\cite{aga_firstcore_2021,chasnupp_lpo_2013}.

A uniform single-batch core, the configuration of this thesis, forgoes
that instrument entirely, which frames the peaking levels measured in
Chapter~\ref{ch:results}.
