\chapter{Traceability of the open data on LABGENE}\label{app:labgene-data}

Table~\ref{tab:labgene-data} lists every item of open information on the
LABGENE prototype used in this thesis, the value adopted, and the primary
source of that value. Items marked as assumptions are modelling choices of
this thesis and carry no claim of correspondence with the prototype.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \footnotesize
  \caption{Open-source data on the LABGENE prototype and their primary
    references.}
  \label{tab:labgene-data}
  \begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.4cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.6cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Item} & \textbf{Value adopted} & \textbf{Primary source} \\
    \midrule
    Reactor type & PWR & \cite{ctmsp_labgene_2015}, confirmed by \cite{vasconcelos_fapesp_2025} \\
    \addlinespace
    Rated power & 48\,MW thermal & 48\,MW stated by \cite{ctmsp_visita_2018}. Thermal per \cite{dinizcosta_2017} and \cite{demartini_nuclep_2022} \\
    \addlinespace
    Fuel & UO$_2$ pellets in rods grouped into fuel elements & \cite{marinha_inb_labgene_2022}, \cite{pereira_rmb_2023} \\
    \addlinespace
    Enrichment class & LEU, below 20\,wt\% & \cite{brazil_npt_wp71_2022}, \cite{brazil_npt_wp13_2026}. Definition of LEU from \cite{nrc_10cfr110_2} \\
    \addlinespace
    Enrichment value & Not fixed. Design space 2.0 to 19.75\,wt\% & Reported 6 to 8\,wt\% \cite{dinizcosta_2017}, 18 to 19\,wt\% \cite{kassenova_2014}, up to 19\,wt\% \cite{vasconcelos_fapesp_2025} \\
    \addlinespace
    Refuelling interval & Objective of the optimisation, not fixed & Four years \cite{galante_podernaval_2020}, five years \cite{dinizcosta_2017} \\
    \addlinespace
    Vessel internal radius & 90\,cm & Figure~13 of \cite{maruyama_2013}, simplified model drawn according to CTMSP \\
    \addlinespace
    Vessel wall and height & Not modelled & 100\,mm and 4700\,mm in the same figure of \cite{maruyama_2013} \\
    \addlinespace
    Active fuel height & 120\,cm, assumption & Estimated from the proportions of Figure~13 of \cite{maruyama_2013} \\
    \addlinespace
    Lattice, pitch, assembly count, absorbers, boron, reflector & Assumptions of this thesis & No open source \\
    \addlinespace
    Status of the plant & Not critical as of May 2025, boiler-steam tests first & \cite{vasconcelos_fapesp_2025}\\
    \addlinespace
    Safeguards status & Special procedures under Article~13 of the Quadripartite Agreement in negotiation & \cite{iaea_dg_statement_2022}, \cite{eu_statement_bog_2025}, \cite{brazil_npt_wp13_2026} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
